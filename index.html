Can AI stop hospitals from throwing away platelets while patients go short in dengue season?

Platelets last only 5 days. This simulation runs a regional blood centre and its hospitals through a season twice, on identical random demand and donations. One run uses current practice and the other uses PlateletIQ, an AI policy that learns demand from a leading fever-surveillance signal and then plans separation, allocation, sharing and donor drives. How PlateletIQ decides, every morning

1 · Sense

Fever signal

Daily count of fever / NS1-positive cases from surveillance. Platelet need follows it about 4 days later, after the critical phase of dengue.

2 · Learn

Online forecaster

Recursive least squares per hospital, pre-trained on last season and updated daily. It predicts demand 0 to 4 days ahead with an uncertainty band.

3 · Produce

Separate to need

Only as many donations are separated into platelets as the forecast needs. The rest stay as red cells and plasma, so nothing is lost.

4 · Allocate

Age-aware + sharing

Each unit goes to a site that will use it before it expires. Near-expiry surplus moves to busier sites, and emergencies draw on neighbours.

5 · Mobilise

Early donor drives

When forecast need outruns expected donations, donor camps are called before shortages appear, not after.

Model (per hospital h, day t)

d̂(h,t+k) = w(t+k) · [ a_h + b_h · f(t+k−4) ] k = 0..4 a_h, b_h ← recursive least squares, forgetting λ = 0.97 σ_h² ← EWMA of squared forecast errors target_h = d̂(t) + 0.35·d̂(t+1) + 0.9·σ_h separate = min(donations, Σ_k≤2 d̂ + z·σ − network stock) drive if Σ d̂(t+3..t+7) > 0.97·expected donations + 0.3·stock

Current practice (baseline): every donation is separated into platelets. Each hospital keeps a fixed par of 2 days' average use (minimum 5), reviewed weekly. Units are issued first-in-first-out, with no visibility of other hospitals' stock. Donor drives are called only after shortages above 3% over 3 days.

Costs used (₹ per unit): expired unit 1,500; patient short 9,000 (emergency apheresis / delayed care proxy); lateral transfer 250; emergency dispatch 600; donor camp 20,000.

Note: hospital names and demand data are synthetic, calibrated to typical Indian district networks. Results show relative policy performance, not clinical outcomes.
